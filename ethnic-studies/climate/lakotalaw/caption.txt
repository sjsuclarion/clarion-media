Say NO to Uranium. Protect Indigenous Land.

On July 30, 2024, two semi-trucks transporting uranium under contract with Energy Fuels, the largest uranium company in the US developing the Pinyon Plain Mine in Arizona, were found crossing tribal lands. A violation of tribal law.

Since its inception, Indigenous peoples have been saying no to uranium. Its effect on local communities has caused cancers, respiratory diseases, and damaged water, livestock, and soils. Abroad, such mining operations have been used to develop weapon technology, and act as a form of imperial power.
